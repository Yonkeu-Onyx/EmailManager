# EmailManager
A gmail Extension that helps you manage your emails
1. The Problem
   Most of us get more emails than we can read. The messages that matter, like a bill, a message from a real person or a security alert, end up buried under promotions, newsletters and notifications. Additionally, emails pile up very fast and most of them are marketing emails, or job alerts that we'll never apply to
or just emails we'll never read.

Sorting through it all takes time and energy. People either skip their inbox for days and miss something important, or spend a chunk of every day clearing it out.

There are tools that help, but many of them are limited.

Who this is for: anyone with a busy inbox who wants a quick, trustworthy picture of what's in it, clean the inbox quickly and efficiently.

2. MVP goals (the first version)

When the first version works, a person should be able to:

Ask for their recent emails, for example "the last 24 hours".
Get a short summary of each email in plain language.
See their emails sorted into a few clear groups: Important, Personal, Updates, Marketing and Other.
Know that their emails stay on their own computer, because the first version uses an AI model that runs locally.
Trust that the tool only reads. It doesn't delete, send or change anything.
3. Long-term goals (after the MVP)

Things I want to add once the first version works well:

A cloud option, so people can choose a more powerful model, and so I can compare it with the local one.
Audio summaries, so you can listen to your day or your week instead of reading it.
Notifications when something important arrives.
